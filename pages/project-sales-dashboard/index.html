Sales & KPI Dashboard

Interactive Power BI dashboard suite for real-time sales tracking, order management, and comprehensive KPI monitoring with drill-through analytics and automated reporting.

Power BI DAX Power Query ETL SQL

🎯 The Challenge

A retail business with multiple sales channels struggled to consolidate their sales data and track performance in real-time. Their challenges included:

  • Data scattered across multiple systems (POS, e-commerce, wholesale)
  • Manual Excel reporting taking 10+ hours weekly
  • Delayed insights leading to missed opportunities
  • No visibility into regional or product-level performance

💡 The Solution

I designed and built a comprehensive Power BI dashboard ecosystem:

  • Data Integration: Power Query pipelines to consolidate data from all sales channels into a unified data model
  • Executive Dashboard: High-level KPI overview with revenue, margins, growth trends, and targets
  • Sales Analysis: Drill-through reports by region, product category, sales rep, and time period
  • Order Tracking: Real-time order status, fulfillment metrics, and delivery performance
  • Advanced DAX: Complex measures for YoY growth, rolling averages, and dynamic targets

⚙️ Key Features

  • Interactive Filters: Slice data by date, region, category, customer segment
